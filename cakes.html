Blueberry Cake

Preparation Time: 15 mins | Bake Time: 30 mins

What you'll need:

  • Measuring Spoons
  • Measuring Cups
  • Whisk
  • Baking Sheets
  • Cake Pan

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of flour
  • 2 cups of blueberries
  • 2 cups of white sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon of pure vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 2 teaspoons of butter
  • 1/2 cup of vegetable oil
  • 1/2 light brown sugar

Preparation

  1. Get your bowl, any bowl, and put your flour in it.
  2. Next, put your baking powder AND baking soda in that same bowl.
  3. Next, whisk it for 10 seconds to ensure everything is mixed.
  4. Next, butter. Grab a small pan to melt your butter, put heat on low, you don't want the butter too hot, just melted.
  5. Next, put both your cups of milk, sugar, oil, eggs, vanilla extract, and salt then mix.
  6. Lastly, toss the blueberries then pour into the cake pan. Put it in the oven for 30 mins, then let it cool for 10 minutes. ENJOY!

Chocolate Cake

Preparation Time: 15 mins | Bake Time: 35 mins

What you'll need:

  • Measuring Spoons
  • Measuring Cups
  • Whisk
  • Baking Sheets
  • Cake Pan

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of brown sugar
  • 1 cup of boiling water
  • 1 teaspoon of espresso powder
  • 1 cup of buttermilk
  • 2 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 of vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup of cocoa powder

Preparation

  1. Get your bowl, any bowl, and put your flour in it.
  2. Mix your flour, cocoa powder, salt, espresso powder, sugar, baking powder and baking soda.
  3. Mix your vanilla, oil, eggs, and buttermilk into the bowl, then mix.
  4. Pour your cake mixture into the cake pan.
  5. After baking, let the cake cool for 10 minutes. ENJOY!
