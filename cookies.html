Chocolate Chip Cookies cookie

Preparation Time: 25 mins | Bake Time: 12-15 mins | Makes about 10-20 depending on size

What you'll need:

  • Measuring Spoons
  • Measuring Cups
  • Spatula
  • Whisk
  • Baking Sheets
  • Big Bowl
  • Small Pan

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of butter (salted/unsalted)
  • 1 cup of granulated sugar
  • 1 cup of Dulce Cana brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons of pure vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 a bag of Semi Sweet Ghirardelli Chocolate Chips
  • 1 bag of Nestle Toll House Mini Morsels Semi-Sweet Chocolate
  • 1 teaspoon of sea salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 3 cups of all purpose flour

Preparation

  1. Get your bowl, any bowl, and put your flour in it.
  2. Next, put your baking powder AND baking soda in that same bowl, mmkay?
  3. Next, whisk it for 10 seconds to ensure everything is mixed.
  4. Next, butter. Grab a small pan to melt your butter, put heat on low, you don't want the butter too hot, just melted.
  5. Next, put both your cups of sugar into the pan with the butter, and mix until fully dissolved.
  6. Next, pour the butter and sugar mixture into the big bowl.
  7. Next, mix everything together with your spatula. (Be aware, you'll be mixing for a long minute until everything is mixed in.)
  8. Your mixture should be sticky, but not dry once fully mix. Add in your chips, than mix again.
  9. Pre-heat your oven to 375 degrees. Grab a baking pan, put your parchment papers on it.
  10. Grab a palm sized amount of cookie dough, and form them into a ball, put it onto the pan, then smash them very slightly to make them look like a cookie.
  11. Lastly, put the cookies into the oven and let them cook for 12 mins. Remove and let it cool for 5 mins. ENJOY!

Maple Pecan Cookies cookie

Preparation Time: 15 mins | Bake Time: 12-15 mins | Makes about 10-20 depending on size

What you'll need:

  • Measuring Spoons
  • Measuring Cups
  • Spatula

Ingredients

  5. Next, put both your cups of sugar and powders into the pan with the butter, and mix until fully dissolved.
  6. Next, pour the butter and sugar mixture into the big bowl.
  7. Add in your syrup and pecans in the mixture.
  8. Next, mix everything together with your spatula.
  9. Pre-heat your oven to 375 degrees. Grab a baking pan, put your parchment papers on it.
  10. Grab a palm sized amount of cookie dough, and form them into a ball, put it onto the pan, then smash them very slightly to make them look like a cookie.
  11. Lastly, put the cookies into the oven and let them cook for 12 mins. Remove and let it cool for 5 mins. ENJOY!
