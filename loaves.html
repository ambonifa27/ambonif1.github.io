Banana Bread

Preparation Time: 15 mins | Bake Time: 50-60 mins

What you'll need:

  • Measuring Spoons
  • Measuring Cups
  • Whisk
  • Baking Sheets
  • Bread Pan

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup of butter (salted/unsalted)
  • 1/2 cup of granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup of Dulce Cana brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon of pure vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 3 large riped bananas
  • Butter spray

Preparation

  1. Get your bowl, any bowl, and put your flour in it.
  2. Next, put your baking powder AND baking soda in that same bowl.
  3. Next, whisk it for 10 seconds to ensure everything is mixed.
  4. Next, butter. Grab a small pan to melt your butter, put heat on low, you don't want the butter too hot, just melted.
  5. Next, put both your cups of sugar into the pan with the butter, and mix until fully dissolved.
  6. Grab a separate bowl to mash your bananas.
  7. Next, pour the butter and sugar mixture into the bowl with the mashed bananas.
  8. Beat your eggs, and mix in your vanilla extract into that same bowl.
  9. Next, mix everything together with your spatula.
  10. Pre-heat your oven to 350 degrees. Grab your bread pan, spray it, then pour your banana bread mixture in.
  11. Lastly, put the bread into the oven and let them cook for 50 mins. Remove and let it cool for 10 mins. ENJOY!

Pumpkin Bread

Preparation Time: 15 mins | Bake Time: 50-60 mins

What you'll need:

  • Measuring Spoons
  • Measuring Cups
  • Whisk
  • Baking Sheets
  • Bread Pan

Ingredients

  • 1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 1/2 cup of granulated sugar
  • 1 15oz pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 teaspoon of pure vanilla extract
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 cups of all purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup of extra light olive oil

Preparation

  1. Get your bowl, any bowl, and put your flour in it.
  2. Next, put your baking powder, sugar, nutmeg and baking soda in that same bowl.
  3. Next, whisk it for 10 seconds to ensure everything is mixed.
  4. Beat your eggs, mix in your vanilla extract, olive oil, and pumpkin puree into the same bowl.
  5. Next, mix everything together with your spatula.
  6. Pre-heat your oven to 350 degrees. Grab your bread pan, spray it, then pour your bread mixture in.
  7. Lastly, put the bread into the oven and let them cook for 50 mins. Remove and let it cool for 10 mins. ENJOY!
