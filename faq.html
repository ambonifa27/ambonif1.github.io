Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to buy the same ingredients provided in the recipe?

No! You can buy any name brand ingredients as long as it's the correct ingredient.

Can I substitute for lower calorie ingredients?

Yes! If you're planning to have a low calorie pastry, Id recommend using substitutes like light brown sugar, or half the amount of sugar to meet your calorie goals!

How long will the baked goods last in the fridge?

No longer than a week. Cookies and cakes can last up to a week to keep it fresh, while loaves can last up to 5 days at its freshest!

Are there going to be any more recipes added anytime soon?

Yes! Recipes will be added soon through recommendations from our Bluebell fans! We will announce when new recipes are added!
